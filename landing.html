About Design Hub

What is Design Hub?

Design Hub is a secure, cloud-based platform built specifically for core engineers who love to design. It acts as a centralized repository where complex 3D geometry can be stored, analyzed, and visualized instantly in a web browser without requiring heavy desktop CAD software.

Our Core Aim

Our mission is to provide a seamless platform where engineers can upload, view, and interact with CAD models globally without the need for heavy desktop software. We aim to foster a collaborative community where designers can share their work, download open-source geometry, and build a verifiable, interactive portfolio to showcase their 3D designs.

The Architect

This is a highly secure, vibe-coded SaaS application engineered by Anubhav Sen. It bridges the gap between modern software practices and core engineering workflows.
